\section*{Introduction}
This report summarizes how work was divided among the four team members for the
\emph{Ensemble Methods: Boosting vs. Bagging} project. Each member owned a distinct
technical track --- Decision Tree, AdaBoost, Random Forest, and Unsupervised/Integration ---
with effort planned and tracked to stay balanced across the team. The tables below present
module ownership, experiments, paper sections, and the overall workload split.

\section*{Collaboration Statement}
All members co-authored the final paper and shared equal writing responsibility. Interfaces
between modules (tree, boosting, bagging, data pipeline) were jointly designed and agreed upon.
Experimental results were reviewed collectively before submission. Workload across
implementation, experiments, testing, and documentation was balanced at approximately 25\% per
member.
